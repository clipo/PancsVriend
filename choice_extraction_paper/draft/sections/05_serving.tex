Two points matter for interpretation.
First, part of the batch spread is amplification by the low temperature: at $T=0.3$ a logit shift is multiplied by about 3.3, so Gemma's swing from 0.027 to 0.94 corresponds to about 1.9 nats in raw logits.
Second, these effects bias sampled estimates as well as logprob reads.
In the seven models whose concurrently sampled tables we kept, only 9\% to 83\% of unsaturated states fell inside the 95\% interval around the sequential exact value, against 95\% expected.
Earlier sampled tables from this project were retired for this reason.
